Chromosome builder

What a tile is. Each tile is a band, a stripe on a stained chromosome that holds hundreds of genes, not a single gene. A real change does not have to match a whole band. It can sit inside one, which is why reports write sub-bands like q11.2, and why a microarray report gives base pair positions instead of bands. The two chromosomes here are a simplified example, not a real chromosome 1 and 2.
