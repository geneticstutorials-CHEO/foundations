How to build it

1
Cut the chromosome. Point at a + in the strip beside a chromosome and a label says where the cut goes, like "cut below q11.2". Click to cut. On a phone, tap the spot, read the label, then press Cut. The red x removes a cut.
2
Move a piece. Drag it onto a chromosome to attach it, leave it on the mat, or drop it on Discard to throw it away.
⟲
Rotate a piece. Tap the rotate button to flip it around.
⧉
Duplicate a piece. Tap the copy button to add a second copy.
✓
Check, classify, explain. Press Check, classify it, then put it in plain words for the patient.
Each stripe is a band. Colours show which chromosome a piece came from. A band moved onto another chromosome, or sitting on the mat, keeps its home chromosome in its label (like 22q12). X and Y sit in the corner. A link mark in the strip shows where two pieces are joined together; a red x shows a cut. A result names a breakpoint by the band the break falls inside. Here a band is a single tile and cannot be split, so your cut goes in the gap between two bands and the whole band moves to one side. Important genes are highlighted beside the band where they sit on the chromosome.
